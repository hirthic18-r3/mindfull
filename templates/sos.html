✳ ONE STEP AT A TIME

For this next moment

  1. 1

    Move closer to another person—a trusted friend, family member, neighbor, or staffed public place.

  2. 2

    Tell them clearly what’s happening. You could say, “I’m not feeling safe alone. Can you stay with me?”

  3. 3

    Put distance between you and anything you could use to hurt yourself. Ask someone else to help if possible.

  4. 4

    Contact emergency services or a crisis line if you might act on these thoughts or can’t stay safe.
