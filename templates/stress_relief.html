GENTLE TOOLS

Pick what feels doable

No perfect way to do these. Skip anything that doesn’t feel right.

2 MIN

Come back to your senses

A grounding exercise to notice what’s around you, one sense at a time.

Start grounding ＋
  1. Name 5 things you can see.
  2. Notice 4 things you can feel against your body.
  3. Listen for 3 things you can hear.
  4. Notice 2 things you can smell.
  5. Name 1 thing you can taste, or one kind thing you can tell yourself.
↗
1 MIN

Unclench & soften

Let your shoulders drop and ease some tension without needing to stretch.

Try a soft reset ＋
  1. Let your feet rest on the floor, if that feels comfortable.
  2. Notice whether your jaw is holding tension; let it soften if you can.
  3. Lift your shoulders gently, then let them fall. Skip this if it hurts.
  4. Take one unforced breath and notice how your body feels now.
✎
3 MIN

Set down a thought

Give a busy thought a place to land, without having to fix it right away.

Reflect gently ＋
  1. What’s taking up the most space in my mind right now?
  2. Is there one small part I can act on today—or can it wait?
  3. What would I say to someone I care about in this moment?
  4. You can keep these thoughts private; nothing here is saved.
